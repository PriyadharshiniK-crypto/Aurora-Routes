About Maldives

The Maldives is a tropical paradise located in the Indian Ocean. It is famous for its crystal-clear blue waters, white sandy beaches, luxury water villas, and colorful coral reefs. The Maldives is one of the most popular honeymoon and vacation destinations in the world. Visitors enjoy activities like snorkeling, scuba diving, island hopping, and relaxing on beautiful beaches.
